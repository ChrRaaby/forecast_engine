- Finding (synthetic only, `tools/exp010.py --power`): the live CDF builder keeps 10% of the mass beyond P10 and beyond P90,
  spread linearly to the bounds, so sharpening narrows only the middle. When made-up outcomes are narrower everywhere (≈3% outside
  the 10–90% range, like our 0 of 19), sharpening wins on most questions but loses as much in the thin tails: mean gain ≈0 at
  N = 40 and 80. Normal-shaped P1/P99 gained ≈+8 baseline points in the same made-up world. So the tail shape (B-15) may be the
  bigger lever. Card v2 open choice 2 recommends adding it as arm B. Caveat: real surprises punish thin tails, and the made-up truth
  has none.
