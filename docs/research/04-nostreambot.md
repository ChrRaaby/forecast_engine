# 04: Lessons from nostreambot (2026-09-28)

**Question:** What can we learn from a strong open-source Metaculus bot?

Repo: https://github.com/No-Stream/nostreambot-metaculus-bot (MIT). Write-ups:
https://nostream.substack.com/p/more-complex-and-maybe-even-better ·
https://nostream.substack.com/p/on-writing-a-bitter-pilled-metaculus

## What it is
- Forked from the Metaculus template, built on `forecasting-tools`, runs on GitHub Actions.
- **Result:** 15th of 277 in the Summer 2026 FutureEval bot tournament (~3,240 spot peer points over 256 questions; the
  leader had ~4,820), above the all-bots ensemble average.
- **Cost:** ~**$2.60/question** live, about 5× our $0.50 cap. The author is actively trying to get it to ~$1.
- **Size:** ~720 Python files / ~280k lines, "~entirely generated by AI with my steering".

## Pipeline (short)
1. Time budget per question: a fast path when close to closing, and it skips questions it can't publish in time.
2. Research in parallel: AskNews (primary), OpenAI native web search, Gemini grounded search, yfinance/FRED,
   **prediction-market prices (Polymarket, Kalshi, Manifold, PredictIt)**, a fetch of the **resolution source named in the
   question**, time-series anchors for numeric questions, and two "gap-fill" passes (list missing facts → search; bounded agentic loop).
3. 3 forecasters, **latest frontier model per vendor** (OpenAI / Anthropic / Google), run independently on the same research.
4. **Median** of the three. Numeric: percentiles → full CDF, then a pointwise median.
5. A publish gate, then a comment carrying each model's reasoning.

## Findings worth taking (their evidence, from docs + FUTURE.md)
- **Median beats the alternatives.** Mean, geometric mean of odds, an LLM "stacker" that rewrites disagreements, and coherence
  weighting were all benchmarked and rejected. The stacker scored no better than the median over 88 questions.
- **A 3-member latest-per-vendor ensemble was non-inferior to 6 members** on binary/MC.
- **Post-hoc calibration was rejected** (Platt, isotonic, shrinkage, extremizing variants): the calibration slope *flips sign
  between eras*, and the fits are underpowered even at N≈400. ⚠️ This contradicts the "calibration is a cheap win" finding in
  research/02. Treat calibration as a hypothesis to test, not a default.
- **The worst misses happen when all models agree on a shared, flawed briefing.** Research quality and diversity is the failure
  point, not aggregation.
- **AskNews contributed only ~16% unique content** in their audit, so they use several sources.
- **Leakage in backtests:** there's no date-restricted search. Instead an LLM **leakage detector** reads each question's research and
  excludes questions whose research reveals the outcome. Research is cached per question so replays don't pay twice.
- **GitHub Actions cron is unreliable:** only ~22% of scheduled firings were delivered (late Aug–early Sep 2026). Fix: multiple cron
  entries, plus an external scheduler (cron-job.org) triggering `workflow_dispatch`. Runs that find no new question cost ~nothing.
- **Donated credits** are kept on a separate OpenRouter key, with guards so they're only spent on Metaculus.
- **Engineering hygiene:** the test suite blocks network access (free to run), there's one file allowed to hold model IDs, and model IDs are
  checked against a live model list rather than written from memory.
- **"Config eras":** every roster/pipeline change is a dated boundary, so results are analysed per era (residual analysis).
- **Design log** of adopted *and rejected* ideas with the evidence (FUTURE.md), the same idea as our `docs/ideas.md`.

## Assessment for forecast_engine
- **Don't fork it.** It's a benchmark and an idea mine, not a base. We couldn't understand or maintain 280k lines of
  AI-generated code on 10 h/week, and its cost profile is 5× our cap.
- **Adopt early, because they're cheap and high-value:** median of 3 diverse-vendor models, prediction-market prices as a research
  input (free APIs), fetching the resolution source, a config-version tag on every forecast, cost logging per
  question, a redundant scheduler, and network-free tests.
- **Adopt at M2:** research cache plus an LLM leakage screen for backtests.
- **Downgrade:** post-hoc calibration moves from "principle" to "experiment".
- **Useful yardstick:** nostreambot's public scores on the same questions give us a free comparison point.
