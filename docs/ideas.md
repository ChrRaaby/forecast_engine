| I-12 | Idea | Graph database for the bot's research, knowledge and reasoning, aiming at (1) better reasoning, (2) transparency for human review, (3) evaluation and learning from past forecasts | **Split the three aims; they have very different evidence.** (2) and (3) are well-founded: nostreambot's most useful tooling is post-mortems on misses and a "which claims were load-bearing" audit. But the value comes from **structured forecast records** (claims → evidence → source + date, base rate, cruxes, each model's probability), not from the database. JSONL/DuckDB is enough until queries genuinely need graph traversal. (1) is the weakest: no evidence yet that a KG improves LLM *forecasting*, and the research points to frontier models + research breadth instead. A KG layer adds extraction cost, latency and errors, and could make the "shared flawed briefing" failure worse by funnelling every model through one graph. **Leakage trap:** a graph that accumulates knowledge over time contaminates backtests unless every node/edge carries a knowledge-date and queries are time-filtered. Watch for hammer-and-nail bias given prior GraphRAG work: prove the need first. **Plan:** M1 persists the raw research + rationales per forecast (cheap). M2/M3 defines a structured forecast-record schema that is graph-loadable. Then load it into a graph for analysis (aim 3) before any attempt at aim 1, which must win an A/B in the backtest. | Parked (prerequisite adopted in M1) | M1 logging; revisit after M2 |
